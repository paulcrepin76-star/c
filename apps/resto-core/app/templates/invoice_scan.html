On the iPhone

  1. Join Tailscale. Open http://100.116.48.120:8088/invoices/scan in Safari.
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app, camera and all.
  3. For a pile: photograph them in Camera first, then pick several from Photos.
  4. For one bill in your hand: take one photo now, then Upload.
  5. Give Paperless a minute to read it. Costing updates by itself — no Sync now needed.
