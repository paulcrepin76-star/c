Text it from your phone

  1. Open Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot, and copy the token.
  2. Put TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN in .env on Unraid, then docker compose up -d grok-bot.
  3. Text your new bot. It answers with your chat id. Put that id in TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_CHAT_IDS and restart it once more.

The server polls Telegram outbound, so nothing has to be open to the internet. Full notes in docs/BOT.md.
