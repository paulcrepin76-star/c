How the sensors get in

The cellar does not log into Govee or steal a camera password. The sensor app, Home Assistant, or n8n posts a reading:

POST /api/house/readings
X-API-Key: (cellar key)
{"fridge": "Walk-in cooler", "temp_f": 37.2}

temp_c also works. Slugs: walk-in-cooler, prep-cooler, line-cooler, pastry-cooler, bar-cooler, wine-cellar, walk-in-freezer.
